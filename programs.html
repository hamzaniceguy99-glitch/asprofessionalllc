Four services for employers, agreed in writing before any work begins.

Program 01 · Three good candidates

Direct hire recruitment

Most bad hires start with a vague brief. We define the role properly — what the person will actually do, what's genuinely required and what's a preference — then source and screen against it and present a short list with notes rather than a pile of CVs.

Program 02 · When you need someone now

Temporary & contract cover

Cover is usually needed at short notice and for a defined period. We arrange it quickly, confirm the engagement terms in writing, and are clear about who employs the worker and who is responsible for what — because that distinction matters.

  • Employers
  • Terms agreed in writing

Program 03 · Checked, with consent

Screening & references

Screening is structured so candidates are compared on the same basis. References are taken properly, and background checks are carried out by third-party providers with candidate consent and in line with the Fair Credit Reporting Act and state law. Hiring decisions stay with you.

Program 04 · The first month

Onboarding support

Plenty of placements fail in the first month for avoidable reasons: nothing prepared, no clear first tasks, nobody checking in. We help put a simple onboarding plan in place and check in with both sides before small problems become resignations.
